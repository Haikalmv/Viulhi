      About these terms

      These terms apply to every purchase made from Viulhi through our website, social media or in person. By placing an order, you agree to them. Please read them before you buy.

      Orders

      An order is confirmed once we've accepted it and received payment. We'll let you know by message or email. We may decline or cancel an order if an item is unavailable or a price was listed in error, and we'll refund anything you've paid in full.

      Prices & GST

      All prices are in Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR). Unless stated otherwise, prices shown on the shop include 8% Goods and Services Tax (GST). Your order summary and invoice show the price before GST, the GST amount and the total separately.

      Delivery fees are shown separately at checkout.

      Payment

      The available payment methods are shown at checkout. If you pay by bank transfer, please include your order number as the reference and send us the transfer slip. We start preparing your order once payment is confirmed.

      Delivery

      • Malé, Hulhumalé and Villimalé: delivered to your door by our delivery partner.
      • Other islands: sent by ferry, speedboat or cargo service to the jetty or contact you choose. Transport charges are paid by the customer.

      Delivery times are estimates. Weather and transport schedules between islands can cause delays that are outside our control. Once a parcel has been handed to an inter-island transport service, we'll share the details so you can track it.

      Handmade items

      Every piece is made by hand, so small differences in size, colour and stitch pattern are normal. Colours may also look slightly different on screen. These differences are part of each item's character, not defects.

      Custom orders

      For custom pieces, we'll agree the design, colours, price and expected completion date with you before we start, and send you a quotation. An advance payment may be required. Because custom items are made just for you, they can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or differ from what we agreed.

      Returns & exchanges

      If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of receiving it with photos, and we'll repair, replace or refund it.

      Ready-made items in unused condition can be exchanged within 7 days. Return delivery costs for exchanges are paid by the customer. Yarn and supplies can only be returned unopened.

      Care & safety

      Toys with small parts such as buttons or safety eyes aren't suitable for children under 3 unless labelled otherwise. Always supervise young children. Follow the care instructions provided with each item: most pieces should be hand-washed in cold water and dried flat in the shade.

      Privacy

      We only use your name, phone number, email and address to process and deliver your order and to contact you about it. We never sell your details. If you agree to hear from us, we may send occasional messages about new products, and you can opt out at any time.

      Changes & governing law

      We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page when you place your order applies to that order. These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Maldives.
